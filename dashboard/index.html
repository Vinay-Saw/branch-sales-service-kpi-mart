Definitions

Year to date
1 January to the end of the last complete month, compared with the same months a year earlier.
Agreement value
Total agreement value of confirmed agreements, by signing date. Cancelled and on-hold agreements are excluded but shown on the Sales tab.
Sales target
Sum of the salespeople's monthly targets. Sales managers carry an overlapping target that is not added on top.
Units installed and target
Units with installation status Completed, by completion date, against the installation engineers' combined "Units completed" target.
Installation backlog
Units on confirmed agreements that are Scheduled, In Progress or Delayed. Units on cancelled or on-hold agreements are parked, not backlog.
Repairs past SLA
Share of repair orders opened in the period whose resolution exceeded the SLA hours for their priority.
Open repair orders
Repair orders with status Open or In Progress today, whatever their age.
AMC renewals due
Active AMC contracts whose end date falls in the next 90 days.
Lapsed AMC
An expired contract with no later contract on the same unit.
Out-of-scale target
A role whose company-wide attainment is below half or above double its target.

Where the numbers come from

Source tables
The CSV extracts in data/ of the branch-sales-service-kpi-mart repository: leads, inquiries, quotations, bookings, units, installation POs, warranty, AMC, repair orders, incentives and employee targets.
Aggregation
dashboard/build_dashboard_data.py turns them into the files listed under Sources. The finding sentences are generated from the same numbers, so they stay correct when the data is rebuilt.
Caveat
The dataset is simulated. Use it to shape the questions and the dashboard, not to claim real rupee impact.
